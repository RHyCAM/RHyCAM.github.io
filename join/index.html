Join us

Join the RHyCAM Lab

We are looking for passionate, curious people to work at the intersection of atmospheric science, hydrology, and civil engineering

As a member of the group, you will help identify key research questions, run model simulations and data-driven analyses, and turn the results into findings that matter to both science and society.

Postdoctoral researchers

We are recruiting a postdoctoral researcher to join RHyCAM starting in January 2027. A detailed position announcement will be posted here when available. Interested candidates may contact Xiaodong with “[Application-Postdoc]” as the email subject line.

Graduate students

Prospective Ph.D. or Master’s students interested in joining RHyCAM should complete the graduate research interest form below. Please provide information about your academic background, research interests, and relevant experience.

Graduate Research Interest Form (opens in a new tab)

Applicants with external fellowships are especially welcome. Relevant opportunities include NSF GRFP, DOE SCGSR, and NASA FINESST.

Current OU graduate students are welcome to inquiry about graduate committees service.

Undergraduate research

OU undergraduates may have opportunities to work on short, independent research projects involving hydroclimate data and modeling.

Students interested in additional research experience are encouraged to explore DOE SULI (national laboratory internships) and NWC REU (summer research opportunities).
