Research / Theme

Infrastructure Resilience

Translating hydroclimate science into engineering decisions

Illustration

Overview

Infrastructure systems are exposed to floods, severe storms, and other hydroclimate hazards. Understanding how these hazards vary and evolve is essential for evaluating risk and making engineering decisions. We connect hydroclimate research with infrastructure risk assessment, resilient design, and planning.

Research directions

  • Translate hydroclimate science into design-relevant hazard metrics and engineering protocols.
  • Assess infrastructure exposure, vulnerability, and risk under individual and compound hazards.
  • Develop stakeholder-informed decision support for infrastructure design, planning, and operations.

What we have learned

Nature Climate Change, 13 (2023)

Wettest Winter Storms in the Western U.S. Growing Wetter

The strongest winter storms in the western U.S. could bring up to 40% more precipitation at storm centers by mid-century. Understanding these changes is important for assessing future storm risks and improving infrastructure planning.
