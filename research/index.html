Our approach

Science guides engineering; engineering asks the questions

Our research brings together scientific understanding and engineering applications. We combine observations, reanalysis, physical modeling, and artificial intelligence to investigate hydroclimate processes, understand extremes, and assess their predictability. At the same time, practical challenges in risk assessment and infrastructure design help shape the scientific questions we ask and the tools we develop.

Research framework

From Climate Variability to Engineering Decisions

We investigate how large-scale climate variability influences regional weather systems, precipitation, and land-surface hydrology. Through prediction, projection, and impact assessment, we connect processes across scales to better understand hydroclimate extremes and inform decisions about water resources, infrastructure design, and resilience.
