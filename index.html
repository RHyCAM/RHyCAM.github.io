Who we are

Science that connects weather, water, and society.

The Regional HydroClimate Analysis and Modeling (RHyCAM) Lab at the University of Oklahoma investigates hydroclimate processes and extremes using observations, numerical modeling, and artificial intelligence. Our work bridges fundamental Earth system science and practical engineering challenges, translating scientific understanding into actionable knowledge for a changing world.

What we have found

Nature Climate Change, 13 (2023)

Wettest Winter Storms in the Western U.S. Growing Wetter

The strongest winter storms in the western U.S. could bring up to 40% more precipitation at storm centers by mid-century. Understanding these changes is important for assessing future storm risks and improving infrastructure planning.
